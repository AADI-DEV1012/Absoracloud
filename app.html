Data Privacy

Privacy Policy

Your privacy is fundamental to our architecture. Learn how AbsoraCloud manages and protects your data.

1. Information We Collect

We collect minimal information necessary to deliver cloud compute services: your username, email address, password hash, and resource usage metrics (compute hours, bandwidth, and storage capacity).

2. How We Use Information

Information is used exclusively for authentication, quota management, abuse prevention, and billing. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third parties.

3. Storage & Cryptography

Passwords are hashed using industry-standard bcrypt encryption. Authentication sessions utilize cryptographically signed JSON Web Tokens (JWT) stored securely on the client.

4. Data Retention and Erasure

You can request complete deletion of your account and associated storage files at any time by contacting our support team. Upon account termination, storage buckets and sandbox files are securely purged.
