Help & Clarity

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I upgrade or downgrade my plan at any time?

Yes! You can upgrade instantly. Quotas update in real-time across all services as soon as your tier changes in the database.

Are there any hidden bandwidth or egress fees?

No. AbsoraCloud does not bill you for inbound or outbound network bandwidth. Your plan price covers your full operational limits.

How are storage quotas calculated?

You get 100% of your allocated storage capacity (e.g. 5GB on Free, 75GB on Pro). Platform runtime logs are handled in a dedicated shared 1GB log pool and are never deducted from your user storage.

Can administrators upgrade users directly via Telegram?

Yes! Authorized administrators can run /upgrade <username> <plan> directly from the AbsoraCloud Telegram Bot to update any account tier immediately.
